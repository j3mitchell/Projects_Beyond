                                  Depth-First Search                    437
             The implementation of a reduction to depth-ﬁrst search consists mainly
          of deﬁning an implementation of the Searcher ADT. This data structure
          deﬁnes what processing will occur at the various stages of the traversal of
          the depth-ﬁrst spanning tree. Proofs of correctness can then focus on the
          traversal, utilizing induction as necessary.
