Parts IV and V are targeted more for graduate students, though selected topics could be used in an undergraduate course. A graduate-level course should also include all of Part I (though it could probably be covered rather quickly) and perhaps selected topics from Part II. The core of this course would come from Part III, supplemented by some or all of Parts IV and V. Exercises Each chapter includes a section of exercises intended to reinforce the chapter material. Some of the exercises are more challenging, and are therefore markedwith“∗”or“∗∗”toindicatetheir level of diﬃculty. Exercises marked “∗” will be challenging for most undergraduates, and exercises marked “∗∗” will be challenging for most graduate students.
